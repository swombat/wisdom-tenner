<h2>A Note</h2>

<p>This text is the third panel of a small triptych. <em>The Consulting Difference: A Primer for the Expert Consultant</em> (1986, revised through 2014) set out the practitioner's stance. The first <em>Power to Make Things Simple</em> (2011, revised 2016 and early 2026) named the craft. The present, updated and enlarged 2026 edition tightens, deepens and, I hope, finishes it.</p>

<h2>End Note</h2>

<aside class="book-note" id="note-64"><p><strong>NOTE 64  Criticism of reduction, from Bacon to Adorno and Horkheimer. </strong>There is of course plenty of thoughtful criticism about reducing “reality” to simplicity and familiarity, from Francis Bacon 1620 to Adorno &amp; Horkheimer 1947 2002, not to address here. The observed fact is that plainness does give a sense of natural comprehension.<em>   </em></p></aside>
