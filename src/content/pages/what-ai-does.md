AI can help this site without becoming its author. Ioan Tenner’s authorship and judgement remain decisive.

This page records material AI contributions in four parts:

1. **Source or question** — what Ioan supplied or asked.
2. **AI contribution** — what an AI collaborator proposed, organised, researched, transformed or implemented.
3. **Verification and correction** — what was checked, what remains uncertain, and what errors were corrected.
4. **Final human choice** — what Ioan accepted, rejected or changed.

The record is not meant to suggest that every mechanical operation is profound. It is meant to make consequential collaboration visible.

## Renewal of this site, 2026

### Source or question

Ioan asked for his existing *Wisdom Sleeping* site to be renewed, updated and enlarged without losing its history, living voice or accumulated writing. The public site had to remain editable by him, and established page addresses were to be preserved unless a deliberate change was agreed.

### AI contribution

AI collaborators helped recover and organise the existing public material, examine naming and structure, propose a maintainable static site, and implement the agreed design and navigation. Sol assembled the present renewal. Fable and Deep contributed independent reflection and criticism during the naming and framing discussion.

### Verification and correction

The imported site is checked for expected pages, working internal links and available local images. Existing public page addresses have been retained, including `/blog.html`, although its visible navigation label is now **Thoughts**.

AI output is not treated as evidence merely because it is fluent. Factual claims, quotations and attributions require checking against appropriate sources; uncertainty and correction should remain visible when they matter.

### Final human choice

Ioan chose the public name **Human Wisdom** with the phrase *made simple*. He approved the description of the site as **a renewed, updated and enlarged 2026 continuation of Ioan Tenner’s _Wisdom Sleeping_**, the broader introductory account of human wisdom, the change from **Blog** to **Thoughts**, and this transparent account of AI collaboration.

Daniel Tenner confirmed the boundary between the private family repository and the public site repository. Ioan confirmed that his own usable editing route was in place and asked the work to proceed.

The writing remains Ioan’s. AI may clarify, question, arrange, check and help make changes; it does not make the final choice in his place.
