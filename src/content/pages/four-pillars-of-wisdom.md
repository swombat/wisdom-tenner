This is my main, ongoing study about worldly wisdom. It persists under continuous development and it will be so for a very long wile, I hope. Do not expect to find the whole essay too soon, I would like to live a little longer; [let my voyage to Ithaca be a long one](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantine_P._Cavafy)... I will add chapters, from time to time. The whole will change at will, by adding and cutting and on-line editing.

Meanwhile, in my "blog" I gather example and comment of what I believe to be wisdom and its opposites; that keeps me busy. It may be that in the end the examples will tell more than the theory.

Moreover I do not write this for clicks or for a career. Wide audience is not a concern so that I can indulge in musing instead of concise communication."Wisdom, sleeping" is a record, one as I would have liked to have from my own parents, for family memory; simple enough to be mind-size, to give thoughts one can apply but not too simple so that living complication and subtlety would be lost; generous, I hope, candid and true to experience but not *politically correct or scientifically correct* so that my own common sense truth and intuition would be lost.
