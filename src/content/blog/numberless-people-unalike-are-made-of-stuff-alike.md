​This is the secret key to knowing people as they are, what they feel, how they may judge, what they may do:

Bet that what you experience, other people would experience too; what you fear, desire, despise or admire, other people do. The main life situations are similar with all humans written into the human nature and the human condition. Because you are of the same make, of the same species, because you live fit to the same world.

We all are the descendants of the same Sapiens ancestors "wired biologically" in the same way, resulting from the one evolution and the same Big History of life on our Earth and of people living among people.

The images and purposes flow in similar ways in our human heads, as we humans are brought up and function similarly, in dissimilar but still comparable cultures.

Third, as long as we share a same language root, we also share the underlying ways of understanding and reasoning built into its structures of related words.

Fourth, with similar language, environment and life-world, even more when we share having faced the same situations, needs, crises, challenges, questions, we also share the choices of responding to them. (Even as we may give them different priority, the list of values we adhere to is still the same list of human values).

If you do not believe my hint that we humans are similar and comparable, read the Essays of Montaigne [1] and chances are that you will see yourself in a mirror.

The human mind has a power of empathy: to mirror, resonate, mimic, identify with, imagine and decipher everything human in a universally shared mental language of common sense. What is human made we are born to comprehend; whatever is felt, conceived and made by humans we are endowed to reconstruct and understand [2]. Intuitively.

With this recipe, you can start, you will not be far from truth, as long as you keep things simple; and your mind open and prudent, to check that your intuition is correct or needs improvement.

The differences - and there certainly are differences, often striking and dramatic and irreducible - come from the Cultures in which we were brought up, the personal histories, special circumstances, roles, identities, interests, obligations and points of view. The most difficult to keep in harmony are those coming from exclusive religions and ideologies. These you can also find out or recognise if you care to inquire and observe.
                                                                              \*

To know people, start with asking yourself what you would experience and do if you were him, her, them, in their situation, by what you know, what they may feel and know or ignore. Then look, listen and judge with common sense and a mind open to permanent change, as **things keep evolving while you think and act**.

Do this and you will become a fine psychologist. Forget the complicated theories.

---

As an example, a useful question of motives is, to ask yourself when you meet individuals and even groups: “At what level is this person right now, on the popular Maslow [3] pyramid of motives?” Then, try to act accordingly.

Basic things push people, or pull them to do what they do. First things act first: fear of death, urge to be safe of pain, fear and loss, need to own things, need of certainties and territory, a place among the other to belong, to communicate, sex, pride, desire for power, hope and the need to believe, being who you truly are, ever better [4] and later if not last, being civilized and good, spirit divine - other than beasts. Very rare are the perverts with an urge of death and a love for evil for evil's sake. Humans want forever more, at least for their children. One need satisfied, another arises. You will never have everything you need or wish. Nobody will.

Count on this without reducing persons to simplicity; your good sense will quickly enrich the basic model with the facts and nuances you know or observe.

​Essential strokes will enrich the portrait when you care, listen, observe, when you enter dialogue and do things together with people.
